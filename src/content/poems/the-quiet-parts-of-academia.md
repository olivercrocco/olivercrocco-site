---
title: "The quiet parts of academia"
date: 2023-03-27
place: "Baton Rouge"
note: "First written in March 2023 as a short prose piece on the old blog. Set in lines in October 2026."
---
I like the quiet parts.
Offices. Libraries.
A window
with the noise
on the other side of it.
Reading.
Thinking,
though thoughts
can be loud.
Writing,
the keys going
and the keys stopping.

The inbox is not quiet.
Keep it closed
and it is.
Answer it
and it gets louder.

What gets written
gets written
in the quiet parts.
