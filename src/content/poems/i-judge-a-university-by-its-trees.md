---
title: "I judge a university by its trees"
date: 2023-04-20
place: "Baton Rouge"
note: "First written in April 2023 and posted on the old blog. Reworked in October 2026."
---
I judge a university by its trees.

They need room
above the ground
and under it.
No one walking past
sees the roots
feel their way
in the dark.

They do not grow fast.
Each day something moves,
a branch out this way,
a branch out that,
and the tree stands.

Most of them
have a season
when they look
as if nothing
could touch them,

and a season
when they look dead.

From my window
on the parade ground,
a bare one in February.
The same one in March.
