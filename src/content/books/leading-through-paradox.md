It reflects both authors' sustained cross-cultural engagement: Crocco's years living, teaching, and conducting fieldwork across Southeast Asia, from community-based organizations on the Thai-Myanmar border to a visiting professorship at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok, and Ghosh's scholarship on developmental relationships and mentoring across cultural contexts."

Co-authored with Rajashi Ghosh (Teachers College, Columbia University). Draws on developmental relationships theory and adult development research to examine how leaders navigate the paradoxes of autonomy and connection, performance and care, and stability and change without resolving them prematurely.
