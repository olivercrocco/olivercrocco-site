The book introduces a three-tier ecosystem framework for understanding HRD across the eleven Southeast Asian nations — covering culture, government, and ASEAN at the macro dimension; education, professional networks, and international organizations at the meso dimension; and workers, organizations, and regional talent mobility at the micro dimension.

Drawing on World Bank Enterprise Surveys, ILO labor force data, UNDP Human Development Index scores, and ASEAN Statistical Yearbook records, the book documents both the convergent dynamics that ASEAN's regional architecture creates and the persistent divergences across CLMV (Cambodia, Lao, Myanmar, Vietnam) and ASEAN-5 country clusters. The book situates HRD as a distinct field within Southeast Asia — between the national and the global — with regional logics that economists, political scientists, and global HRD scholarship have largely missed.
